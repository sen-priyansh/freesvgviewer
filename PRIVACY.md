# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

This policy covers the Free SVG Viewer website and web app. It explains what happens to files you open, what the app stores in your browser, and what happens when you use an external link.

## Your files

Free SVG Viewer processes SVG files in your browser. When you select or drop a file into the app, it is read locally to show a preview, inspect the SVG markup, or create an export.

The app does not have a file-upload service. Your SVG is not sent to Free SVG Viewer for viewing, conversion, or storage. PNG and ICO exports are created in the browser too.

We do not keep a gallery, file history, or copy of the SVGs and exports you work with. Closing the tab, refreshing the page, or choosing another file removes the current file from the app.

Your browser will still make ordinary requests for the website itself, such as the page, scripts, styles, icons, and updates. Those requests do not include the SVG file you choose in the app.

## Cookies and tracking

Free SVG Viewer does not use cookies for advertising, analytics, login, or tracking. It does not include analytics products, tracking pixels, session-replay tools, or a newsletter signup.

We do not collect a record of SVG filenames, exports, or editor activity. The app has no accounts and no conversion history attached to a user.

## Browser storage and offline use

The app uses browser-managed storage for offline use:

- **Offline cache:** A service worker may cache site files such as pages, scripts, styles, and icons so the app can work offline. Selected SVG files and exported images are not deliberately added to that cache.

You can remove this information at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Doing so may also remove the offline version of the app.

## Technical information

Your browser and the company that hosts or delivers the website may handle normal technical information needed to load a webpage, including an IP address, browser type, request time, and requested page. This is separate from the app's local processing of your SVG file.

Browser extensions, security software, and network administrators may have their own policies and visibility. Their handling of information is outside the control of Free SVG Viewer.

## External links

The app links to the public [Free SVG Viewer GitHub repository](https://github.com/sen-priyansh/freesvgviewer). If you follow a link to GitHub, you leave this site and GitHub's privacy policy applies to your activity there.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is a file-viewing tool and does not provide accounts, comments, or direct messaging.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes or when a clearer explanation is needed. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. If a future change would affect how files or personal information are handled, this page will be updated to say so.

## Questions

If you have a privacy question or find a mismatch between this policy and the app's behavior, please open an issue on the [project's GitHub issues page](https://github.com/sen-priyansh/freesvgviewer/issues).
